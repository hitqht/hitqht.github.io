Qi Hongtuo, Ph.D., is a Distinguished Research Fellow at the School of Civil Engineering, Chongqing University, a professor-level senior engineer, and a National Class-1 Registered Structural Engineer.
He has worked on intelligent construction in both research and practice at the HIT Architectural Design Institute, Wanda Commercial Real Estate, Zhongke Daye Construction Technology, and Huazi Construction Group, and has been on the faculty of Chongqing University since December 2021.

He is PI of four national-level projects (including a National Key R&D Program topic and NSFC grants) and has received two provincial/ministerial science and technology awards.
In the past three years he has published 25 SCI papers as first or corresponding author, and has authored three scholarly monographs and one textbook.

**Research keywords:** prefabricated construction, domain-specific large models for construction, intelligent design, intelligent inspection, construction robotics.
