1. **Prefabricated construction**: prefabricated high-rise steel–concrete hybrid structural systems and EPC project delivery.
2. **Domain-specific LLMs for construction**: key technologies of large models for building quality and safety supervision.
3. **Intelligent design**: shear-wall structures, residential layouts, and wind-turbine towers via deep learning and multi-objective optimization.
4. **Intelligent inspection**: recognition of concrete surface defects; flatness acceptance with terrestrial laser scanning.
5. **Construction robotics**: ceiling-panel installation robots; 3D printing of complex precast components.
